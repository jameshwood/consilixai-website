Consilix brand, September 2026

Stone and petrol.

A slight rebrand: same wordmark, same serif, same quiet layout. The paper goes from cream to a cool stone, the accent goes from burnt sienna to petrol, and the near-black loses its warmth. Everything that made the site look like Anthropic's is gone; everything that made it Consilix stays.

Why

The overlap that had to go

Three of the four old tokens sat within a few points of Anthropic's public palette, and Fraunces reads as a close cousin of their serif. Changing only the accent would have left cream-plus-black-plus-serif, which is the part people recognise. So the ground moved too.

Tokens

The system

Every value below is live in app/assets/stylesheets/application.css on the website and mirrored in the toolkit's globals.css. Names did not change, so nothing else in either codebase needed touching.

paper#F0F1EDPage ground. A grey-green stone, not a cream.
paper-2#E7E9E3Cards, panels, alternate sections.
paper-3#DCDFD7Hover states, pills.
ink#14181AText, buttons, dark sections. Cool, not warm.
ink-mute#5B6367Secondary text. Biased toward the accent hue.
accent#0E5C63The logo dot, kickers, active states, one line of the hero.
accent-deep#093F45Hover on accent.
accent-bright#2F8F98The accent on ink surfaces, where petrol would vanish.

Type is unchanged: Fraunces for display, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for values. The wordmark is Fraunces at optical size 96, weight 600, which is what the original PNG was cut in.

Logo

Re-cut as vector

The only logo files that existed were PNGs, which the magazine cannot print. The wordmark and the mark have been rebuilt from the Fraunces outlines as SVG and PDF, with the dot in petrol. Files are in the website repo and in the magazine print-assets folder.

Alternatives

Two other dots, considered

Both work with the stone ground and the serif. Petrol is recommended because it reads as engineered and calm, which is the whole pitch, and because no consultancy in the magazine owns it. Cobalt reads as finance; forest reads as sustainability, which the magazine already leans on. Switching is one token and a re-export of the logo.
